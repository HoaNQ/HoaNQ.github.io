Privacy Policy — Olympiad Math

Effective date: 28 November 2026

This policy covers the app Olympiad Math: Grades 1–8 (bundle ID vn.annam.imo), published by Nguyen Quang Hoa for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android. The app helps students in grades 1–8 practise olympiad-style multiple-choice math questions offline.

Summary

The app needs no account and has no server of its own that receives your data. All questions are packaged inside the app. Progress and settings are stored on the device. The free version on iPhone, iPad and Android may show Google AdMob ads in a mode intended for children; the Mac version has no ads. The one-time "Remove ads" purchase is handled by Apple or Google Play.

Data stored on the device

  • the grade you chose, your answers, scores, mock-test results, saved questions, review schedule, daily-challenge streak and achievements;
  • settings (theme, sounds, text size, reminders);
  • whether "Remove ads" was bought.

The publisher does not receive any of this. Deleting the app deletes it. A backup file is only created when you choose "Back up progress", and it goes only where you send it.

The app does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos, and it does not ask for a name, age or email address.

Ads (free version on iPhone, iPad, Android)

The free version uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show banner ads outside the question and test screens and an occasional full-screen ad when you leave a result screen. Every ad request is marked as directed to children (child-directed treatment) with the maximum ad content rating "G"; for such requests Google does not serve personalised ads. The app does not ask for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency). Google may still process limited technical data, such as the IP address (to estimate a rough location), device type and ad interactions, to deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK), Google's consent form is shown first; it can be opened again from Settings › Ads › Ad privacy in the app.

After "Remove ads" is bought, the app does not start the ads SDK.

More: Google Privacy & Terms, Google's families ad policies.

In-app purchase

"Remove ads" is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google Play. The publisher never receives card numbers, bank details or your email address. The app only receives the status of the purchase from the store, to unlock or restore it, and keeps that status on the device. Purchases and external links are behind a parent check.

Sharing

The publisher does not sell or share personal data. Data may only be processed by Google (ads, free version) and by Apple or Google Play (payments, and crash reports if you allow sharing with developers), under their own policies.

Children

The app is made for children and their parents. It collects no personal information from children, has no chat, no profiles and no social features, and shows only child-directed, non-personalised ads in the free version.

Keeping and deleting data

All data stays on the device until you delete the app or reset progress in Settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and its effective date will be published at this address.
