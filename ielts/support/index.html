Support — IELTS Listen & Read Practice

IELTS Listen & Read Practice ("IELTS Prep") offers 300+ IELTS Reading and Listening practice tests with band scores, answer keys and Listening transcripts, on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android. No account is needed; progress is stored on your device.

Frequently asked questions

Listening audio does not play

Listening audio streams from the content provider's server and needs an internet connection. Turn on Wi-Fi or mobile data and tap play again. Reading tests and everything else work offline.

How do I restore IELTS Pro on a new device or after reinstalling?

IELTS Pro is a one-time purchase tied to the account that bought it: your Apple Account (iPhone, iPad, Mac) or your Google account on Google Play (Android). Sign in to the store with that account, open the app, then go to Profile › Restore Purchases (also on the IELTS Pro screen). An internet connection is required. App Store and Google Play purchases are separate: a purchase on Android does not unlock Pro on iPhone, iPad or Mac, and vice versa.

What is free and what does Pro add?

Several tests in every collection are free. IELTS Pro unlocks the full library of 300+ tests and removes ads. No subscription, no recurring fees.

Can I move my progress to another device?

Progress is stored on the device and is not synced between devices. Your device backup (iCloud/Finder or Google backup) can include it, depending on your backup settings.

Ads and privacy

The free version on iPhone, iPad and Android shows Google AdMob ads in the test list and after a finished test, never during a test. See the Privacy Policy.
