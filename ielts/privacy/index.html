Privacy Policy — IELTS Listen & Read Practice

Effective date: November 21, 2026

This policy applies to IELTS Listen & Read Practice (shown as "IELTS Prep" under the app icon; bundle ID and Android application ID vn.annam.ieltsprep), published by Nguyen Quang Hoa on the App Store (iPhone, iPad and Mac) and on Google Play (Android phones and tablets).

Summary

  • No account is needed and the publisher runs no server that receives your data.
  • Practice tests ship inside the app. Your progress and settings are stored on your device.
  • Listening audio is streamed over HTTPS from the content provider's server.
  • The free version on iPhone, iPad and Android shows Google AdMob ads. The Mac version shows no ads.
  • The one-time "IELTS Pro" purchase is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play).
  • The publisher does not sell personal data.

Data stored on your device

  • test attempts, answers, band scores, statistics, streak, daily goal and exam date;
  • the display name and avatar emoji you choose (optional, never sent anywhere);
  • settings such as theme, sound, reading font and layout, and the daily reminder time;
  • "IELTS Pro" purchase status and your ad choices.

The progress database is encrypted on the device. The publisher does not receive this data. Deleting the app deletes it. Device backups (iCloud/Finder on Apple devices, Google backup on Android) may include your progress and settings, according to your backup settings.

The daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on the device; no server is involved. The app does not use the camera, microphone, location services, contacts or photos.

Listening audio

When you play a Listening test, the app downloads the audio over HTTPS from the content provider's server (ieltstrainingonline.com). Like any web request, that server receives your IP address and basic technical details of the request. The app sends no name, account or progress with it. Without an internet connection, audio does not play; everything else works offline.

Advertising and consent (free version on iPhone, iPad and Android)

The free version uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to load, show and measure ads, prevent fraud and operate the ad service. Ads appear in the test list (banner) and after you finish a test (full screen). No ads are shown while you take a test.

Depending on your device, region and choices, Google and its partners may process device and advertising identifiers (for example the Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA), advertising data and ad interactions, approximate location derived from your IP address, and performance, diagnostic and crash data.

  • Where required (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app shows Google's consent form before requesting ads.
  • On iPhone and iPad the app asks for App Tracking Transparency permission. You can decline and still use the app; ads are then not personalized. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Google / Privacy › Ads settings.
  • After "IELTS Pro" is purchased, the app does not initialize the ads SDK.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In-app purchase

"IELTS Pro" is a one-time, non-consumable purchase processed by Apple or Google Play. The publisher never receives your card, bank or payment details. The app receives only the transaction status from the store to unlock or restore Pro and stores that status on the device. See Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Sharing with third parties

Data is processed only by Google (ads, free version), Apple or Google Play (payments, and crash reports if you choose to share them with developers), and the audio host (Listening streams), as described above and under their own policies.

Children

The app is intended for IELTS candidates and is not directed at children under 13. It does not ask for age or email address and creates no online profile. Parents or guardians with questions can contact us.

Retention and deletion

Data in the app stays until you delete it in the app or delete the app. Because there is no account or server, the publisher holds no copy of your data to delete. Advertising and tracking choices can be changed at any time in system settings.

Changes

When features, SDKs or legal requirements change, this policy is updated and the effective date above changes.
