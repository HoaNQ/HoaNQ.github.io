Privacy Policy — SAT Prep

Effective date: November 21, 2026

This policy covers the SAT Prep: Digital SAT Practice app (name under the icon: SAT Prep; iOS bundle ID com.quanghoa.sat, Android application ID annam.vn.sat), published by Nguyen Quang Hoa on the App Store (iPhone, iPad) and Google Play (Android phones and tablets).

Summary

  • No account or sign-in. The publisher runs no server that receives your study progress.
  • The question bank is bundled in the app. Your progress and settings stay on your device.
  • The free version shows Google AdMob ads. Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Apple or Google.
  • The publisher does not sell personal data.

Data stored on your device

  • study plan (target score, test date, daily goal) and settings (theme, text size, reminder time);
  • answers, results, bookmarks, review schedule, mock exam results, streaks and achievements;
  • Pro purchase status.

The publisher does not receive this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it. Device backups made by Apple (iCloud/Finder) or Google (Android backup) may include your progress and settings, depending on your backup settings; on Android the bundled question bank is excluded from backups.

Advertising (free version)

The free version uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to load, show and measure ads, prevent fraud and operate the ad service. Ads appear only between activities (for example after a practice session) and as optional rewarded ads; never while you answer a question or take a timed mock exam.

  • Google may process device identifiers (on Android the Advertising ID, on iOS the IDFA only if you allow tracking), app and ad interactions, approximate location derived from your IP address, and performance, diagnostic and crash data, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Data is encrypted in transit.
  • iPhone and iPad: the app asks once for App Tracking Transparency permission. If you decline, the IDFA is not used and the app works the same.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Google settings.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form (UMP) before requesting ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy choices.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps.

In-app purchase

SAT Prep Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. On iPhone and iPad it is processed by Apple through the App Store; on Android by Google through Google Play Billing. The publisher receives no card, bank or payment details; the app only receives the purchase status from the store to unlock or restore Pro.

Online content the app loads

The app needs an Internet connection. Besides ads and the store, it loads, over HTTPS:

  • text fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com);
  • math fonts for formula rendering from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net);
  • the Desmos graphing calculator (www.desmos.com) when you open it during a mock exam.

Like any web request, these servers receive your IP address and basic technical information. The app does not send your name, account or study progress to them.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for notification permission and schedules a local notification on your device (on Android it is rescheduled after the device restarts). Nothing is sent to a server. You can turn reminders or the permission off at any time.

Children

SAT Prep is intended for high-school students preparing for the SAT and adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and the publisher does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices and deletion

Because there is no account or server, the publisher holds no copy of your data to delete; uninstalling the app removes it from your device. Ad-related data processed by Google is managed under Google's policies and controls.

Changes

We will update this page and its effective date when the app's data practices change.
