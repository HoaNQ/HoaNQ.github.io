Support — SAT Prep

SAT Prep helps you prepare for the Digital SAT on iPhone, iPad and Android: practice by skill, full-length adaptive mock exams, spaced-repetition review and progress stats. No account is needed; your progress is stored on your device.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the app say "No Internet connection"?

The app needs a connection to load fonts, math rendering and ads. Turn on Wi-Fi or mobile data; the app continues automatically when the connection returns.

I bought Pro. How do I restore it?

Open Settings › Upgrade to Pro and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account (iPhone/iPad) or Google account in Google Play (Android) you used to buy. On Android, Pro is also restored automatically when the app starts.

My purchase says "pending".

Some payment methods (or Ask to Buy on Apple devices) need approval first. Pro unlocks automatically once the payment is approved; keep the app installed.

How do refunds work?

Refunds are handled by the store: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google in your Google Play order history.

The daily reminder doesn't appear.

Allow notifications for SAT Prep in your device settings, then turn the reminder off and on again in Settings › Notifications. On Android, the reminder may arrive a few minutes late to save battery.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, open Settings › Privacy choices. On Android you can also manage ad personalization in your device's Google settings; on iPhone/iPad in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Does uninstalling delete my progress?

Yes. Progress is stored only on your device (and in device backups, if enabled).

I found a mistake in a question.

Email us the question text or a screenshot and we will check it.
